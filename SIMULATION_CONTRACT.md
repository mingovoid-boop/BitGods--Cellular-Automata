# BitGods Simulation Contract v1

1. State has exactly one authoritative subsystem owner.
2. Actions require registered capabilities.
3. World changes occur through transformations.
4. Important changes emit structured events.
5. Events preserve causal links where available.
6. Knowledge belongs to agents, artifacts, or institutions; it is not globally unlocked.
7. Matter/resources do not teleport between inventories or networks.
8. Infrastructure exposes capacity, condition, dependencies, and flow.
9. Agents can hold incomplete or incorrect beliefs while world truth remains authoritative.
10. Civilizations are recognized persistent patterns, not arbitrary spawn objects.
11. History persists through durable events, artifacts, structures, and records.
12. Distant simulation may aggregate.
13. Aggregation must preserve important causal state and identities.
14. Rendering/UI are views and command surfaces, not authoritative state.
15. Player and autonomous agents operate through compatible world rules.
