# BitGods Alpha Playtest

## Launch
Windows: run `alpha/launch_windows.bat`
Linux/macOS: run `alpha/launch_linux_mac.sh`
Then open `http://localhost:8080`.

## 30–60 minute route
1. Move and inspect terrain.
2. Gather wood and stone.
3. Use Cell Craft.
4. Find/observe autonomous Bitfolk.
5. Trigger or encounter a world problem.
6. Fight a creature and inspect loot.
7. Inspect settlement/economy visuals.
8. Try Avatar / Architect / God modes.
9. DNA Scan.
10. Trade / Political overlays.
11. Fork a timeline.
12. Save, refresh/exit, reload.
13. Record anything confusing, broken, boring or unexpectedly fun.

## Do not judge only feature presence
Record:
- what you understood without explanation
- what felt fun
- what felt pointless
- UI friction
- simulation behavior that looked fake
- bugs
- performance problems
- things you wanted to do but could not
