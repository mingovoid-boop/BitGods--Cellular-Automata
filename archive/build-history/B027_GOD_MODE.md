# B027 BitGod Mode

B027 adds the counterfactual contract: snapshot a world state, fork it, intervene in one branch, run both branches, and compare outcomes.

Interventions are not free outcome selectors. The rain intervention improves water availability but also creates flood damage. The causal debugger records both beneficial and harmful downstream effects.

God Mode inspectors span cell → planet and expose state, causes, consequences, dependencies, history, knowledge and networks.
