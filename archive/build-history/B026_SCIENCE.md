# B026 Science, Genetics, Atomic & Quantum

Technology is produced through evidence and instrumentation rather than a global unlock bar.

The DNA edit test improves cold tolerance while reducing growth and water efficiency, proving edits can create tradeoffs.

The quantum portion is intentionally a small, normalized two-state educational abstraction. It does not claim to simulate arbitrary macroscopic quantum fields. Fictional mechanics are separately classified as EXOTIC_FICTIONAL.
